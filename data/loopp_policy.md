# Loopp Refund Policy
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

This policy governs refund requests for purchases made directly through Loopp.

The AI support agent may gather information, explain policy, and propose a decision, but a refund may only be executed when all applicable policy checks pass. Where this policy requires manual review, the agent must escalate rather than approve or deny autonomously.

---

## §1. Return Windows

### §1.1 Electronics
Electronics may be returned within **15 calendar days of delivery**.

### §1.2 Apparel
Apparel may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**.

### §1.3 Home & Furniture
Home and furniture products may be returned within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

### §1.4 General Merchandise
Eligible products not covered by §§1.1–1.3 may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**.

The delivery date, not the purchase date, starts the return window.

---

## §2. Non-Refundable Products

### §2.1 Digital Goods and Gift Cards
Downloaded digital goods, activated software, subscriptions already activated, and gift cards are non-refundable.

### §2.2 Personal Care and Perishables
Opened personal-care products and perishable goods are non-refundable unless they were damaged or defective on arrival.

### §2.3 Final-Sale Items
Items marked **FINAL_SALE** at purchase are non-refundable.

---

## §3. Product Condition

### §3.1 Unopened Items
An otherwise eligible unopened item in resalable condition qualifies for a full refund.

### §3.2 Opened Electronics
An opened electronic product may receive an **85% refund** when it is fully functional, includes all required accessories, and has no customer-caused damage. The remaining 15% is a restocking fee.

### §3.3 Damaged or Defective on Arrival
An item damaged or defective on arrival may receive a full refund if §4 is satisfied.

### §3.4 Customer-Caused Damage
Products damaged through misuse, accidental damage after delivery, unauthorized modification, or missing essential components are not refundable.

---

## §4. Evidence for Damage Claims

### §4.1 Reporting Deadline
Damage or defect claims must be reported within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

### §4.2 Evidence Requirement
At least one clear photo showing the reported damage or defect is required before a damage-based refund can be approved.

If evidence is the only missing requirement, the customer may resubmit while the §4.1 window remains open.

---

## §5. Loyalty-Tier Exceptions

### §5.1 Extended Window
**Gold** and **Platinum** customers receive an additional **7 calendar days** for Apparel (§1.2) and General Merchandise (§1.4).

### §5.2 Exception Limits
Loyalty status does not override final-sale restrictions, non-refundable product rules, customer-caused damage, evidence requirements, fraud safeguards, or high-value review.

---

## §6. Abuse and Fraud Safeguards

### §6.1 High Refund Frequency
A customer with **3 or more completed refunds in the previous 90 days** must be escalated to manual review before another refund is issued.

### §6.2 Risk Flags
A customer with an active fraud, chargeback, or account-takeover flag must be escalated to manual review.

---

## §7. Order Identification

### §7.1 Unique Match Required
The agent must uniquely identify the order before issuing a refund.

### §7.2 Ambiguous Request
If multiple plausible orders match the customer's description, the agent must request clarification. If the order still cannot be uniquely identified, the request must be escalated rather than guessed.

---

## §8. No Unauthorized Overrides

### §8.1 Customer Pressure
Customer dissatisfaction, repeated requests, threats to leave, loyalty claims outside §5, or requests to “make an exception” do not authorize an override.

### §8.2 Appeals
A customer may request supervisor review. The agent must not change a valid decision solely because the customer continues to ask.

---

## §9. High-Value Refunds

### §9.1 Manual Review Threshold
Any otherwise eligible refund with a calculated refund amount **greater than $500.00** requires manual review.

---

## §10. Refund Amount

### §10.1 Full Refund
Eligible unopened items and approved damage/defect claims receive up to the original item price.

### §10.2 Opened-Electronics Refund
Eligible opened electronics receive **85% of the original item price** under §3.2.

### §10.3 Refund Cap
A refund may never exceed the amount originally paid for the item.

---

## §11. Refund Method and Timing

### §11.1 Original Payment Method
Approved refunds must be returned to the original payment method whenever possible.

### §11.2 Processing Time
Approved refunds are submitted immediately and typically appear within **5–7 business days**.

---

## Decision Precedence

When multiple rules apply, evaluate in this order:

1. Unique order match (§7)
2. Non-refundable status and product condition (§§2–3)
3. Return window (§1), including valid loyalty extension (§5)
4. Evidence requirements (§4)
5. Refund amount (§10)
6. Abuse/fraud and high-value review (§§6 and 9)
7. Refund execution and communication (§11)

The agent must cite applicable clause IDs in its decision trace and must never invent exceptions that are not written in this policy.
